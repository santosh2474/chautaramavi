\## 📌 About the School



\*\*Shree Chautara Secondary School\*\* is an educational institution located at Dandakharka, Sunkoshi Rural Municipality–5, Okhaldhunga, Koshi Province, Nepal.



The school was established in \*\*B.S. 2045\*\* and has gradually expanded its academic programs from the initial school level to secondary education and technical education.



Currently, the school provides formal school education from \*\*Grade 1 to Grade 12\*\* and also operates the \*\*Diploma in Computer Engineering\*\* program affiliated with the \*\*Council for Technical Education and Vocational Training (CTEVT)\*\*.



The school focuses on:



\- Quality and accessible education

\- Technology-friendly teaching and learning

\- Practical and student-centered education

\- Discipline and academic excellence

\- Development of skilled human resources

\- Digital transformation in education

\## 🏛️ School History



Shree Chautara Secondary School was established in \*\*B.S. 2045\*\*.



The school was named after the traditional \*\*Chautara\*\* located in the Dandakharka area, which historically served as a resting place for travelers.



The school gradually expanded its educational level:



\- \*\*B.S. 2045\*\* — School established

\- \*\*B.S. 2052\*\* — Grade 6 introduced

\- Gradual expansion up to Grade 8

\- \*\*B.S. 2065\*\* — Grade 9 introduced

\- \*\*B.S. 2066\*\* — Grade 10 introduced

\- \*\*B.S. 2078\*\* — Grade 11 and Diploma in Computer Engineering introduced



The institution continues to work toward providing quality, technology-oriented and accessible education to students.
